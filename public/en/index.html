Surf lessons in Mirleft

Mirleft Surfers is a local surf school in Mirleft, on Morocco's southern coast. First lesson or ready to progress, we pick the beach for the day's waves and take care of the rest.

How a lesson works

  1. On the sand: warm-up, safety, and how to pop up on the board.
  2. In the water: you ride the whitewater with your instructor beside you.
  3. At your pace: the lesson adapts to your level and the day's conditions.

Why Mirleft

Mirleft is a quiet small town between Tiznit and Sidi Ifni, with several beaches along a few kilometres of coast. Fewer people in the water than in Taghazout, and waves that suit beginners as well as experienced surfers.
